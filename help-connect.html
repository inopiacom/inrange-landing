Helping connect glucose data

Someone you care about is using inRange and asked for help getting their glucose data into the app. It takes a couple of minutes, and you do it together on their phone — you never have to share a password over text.

What you'll need

  • Their phone, with the inRange app open on the glucose step.
  • Glucose data that already reaches Apple Health on that phone — or, if they use one, the login for their own glucose-data sharing account or Nightscout site.

Steps

  1. On their phone, in inRange, go to the glucose step.
  2. Tap “Connect Apple Health” and allow inRange to read glucose. This is usually the simplest route.
  3. Using a sharing account or Nightscout instead? Choose that option and sign in. Login details are stored securely in the phone's Keychain — only on that device.
  4. Within a few minutes their glucose graph lights up.

inRange does not connect to, control or communicate with any medical device. It only reads data that is already available in Apple Health or in an account the user chooses to connect.

Your privacy

inRange is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Login details are kept only in the device's secure Keychain — inRange's servers never store them. See our Privacy Policy for details.
