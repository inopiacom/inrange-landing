Questions people ask

Is this a medical device?

No. inRange is a wellness companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, recommend insulin doses, provide critical alerts or replace professional medical advice. AI insights may be inaccurate. Never use inRange for treatment or emergency decisions — follow your care plan and your healthcare team.

Which glucose data works with inRange?

inRange works with glucose and insulin data you already have in Apple Health or Nightscout, and with supported glucose-data sharing accounts you choose to connect. Apple Health also brings in everyday context such as activity, sleep and heart rate, and you can always log values manually. Available data varies by setup.

inRange does not connect to, control or communicate with any medical device.

What do my supporters see?

Only what you choose to share, and only with people you invite. Followers can see your progress, cheer you on, join challenges and talk to your coach to learn how to support you. You can remove a follower at any time.

What does it cost?

inRange is free to download, and you can try each Premium feature once for free. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store — see pricing above. One subscription covers your circle: the people who follow you get Premium too.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Stockholm), for everyone. Encrypted at rest and in transit. Never sold to advertisers or third parties. You can delete everything from the app. See our privacy policy for the details.
