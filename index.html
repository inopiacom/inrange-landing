Questions people ask

Is this a medical device?

No. InRange AI is a wellness and coaching product. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace your healthcare team. It doesn't recommend insulin doses. Always follow your doctor's guidance.

Which sensors do you support?

Dexcom G6 and G7 (via Dexcom Share), FreeStyle Libre (via LibreLinkUp), Medtrum EasySense and EasyPatch (via EasyView, Europe only), and Eversense (via Eversense DMS). Manual fingerstick entries work too, and Apple Health lets you pull in anything else HealthKit knows about. You connect with your own sensor login, and it is stored only on your phone — never on our servers.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Stockholm), for everyone. Encrypted at rest and in transit. Never sold to advertisers or third parties. You can delete everything with one tap and we mean it. A US region is on the roadmap — it isn't live yet, so we won't promise you a date.

What does it cost?

InRange is free at launch — the whole app, no subscription, no card. Paid plans are planned for a later release, and we'll publish what they cost before anyone is ever asked to pay.

Android?

iOS first. Android is planned for 2027 based on how iOS goes. If you're on Android and want to be counted, join the waitlist with a note — it helps us prioritize.
