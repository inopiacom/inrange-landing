Challenges, rewards & milestones

Time in Range stays in focus. Take on challenges suggested to fit you, earn achievements, new levels and milestones, and get weekly stats that celebrate every win — without any pressure to be perfect.

Your own cheer squad

Invite family and friends with an invite code to follow your journey, cheer you on and challenge you. Supporters get a front-row seat and learn what life with diabetes is really like. Sharing is always your choice.

A personal AI coach — shared

A friendly AI wellness coach that gets to know you through the context you choose to share. Chat privately, discover patterns, personal insights and practical wellness tips. Your followers can talk to the same coach to learn how to support you. One coach, shared understanding.

Snap a meal, get a carb estimate

Take a photo for an AI-assisted estimate of your meal's carbohydrates. Estimates may be inaccurate and must never be used for insulin dosing or treatment decisions.

Apple Health & more data sources

See glucose trends and Time in Range at a glance. inRange works with glucose and insulin data you already have in online data sources such as Apple Health and Nightscout, and Apple Health adds workouts, sleep and heart-rate context. It does not connect to, control or communicate with any medical device.

Apple Watch, right there with you

Check your latest status and trends, chat with your coach, log moments and add watch-face complications — including Time in Range — for quick access.

Questions people ask

Is this a medical device?

No. inRange is a wellness companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, recommend insulin doses, provide critical alerts or replace professional medical advice. AI insights may be inaccurate. Never use inRange for treatment or emergency decisions — follow your care plan and your healthcare team.

Which glucose data works with inRange?

inRange works with glucose and insulin data you already have in online data sources such as Apple Health and Nightscout, and with supported glucose-data sharing accounts you choose to connect. Apple Health also brings in everyday context such as activity, sleep and heart rate, and you can always log values manually. Available data varies by setup.

inRange does not connect to, control or communicate with any medical device.

What do my supporters see?

Only what you choose to share, and only with people you invite — share an invite code from the app. Followers can see your progress, cheer you on, suggest and join challenges and talk to your coach to learn how to support you. You can remove a follower at any time.

What does it cost?

inRange is free to download, and you can try each Premium feature once for free. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store — see pricing above. One subscription covers your circle: the people who follow you get Premium too.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Stockholm), for everyone. Encrypted at rest and in transit. Never sold to advertisers or third parties. You can delete everything from the app. See our privacy policy for the details.
